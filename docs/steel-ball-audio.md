# Steel Ball electricity and thunder audio

Original procedural sounds generated by `python3 scripts/generate-steel-ball-audio.py` (Python standard library only, no external samples). Assets live in `public/assets/jolens-blast-skin-assets/audio/`; reproducible hashes are in `steel-ball-audio-sha256.json`.

- Aim: quiet short electrical charge, one fixed take.
- Launch: sharp electrical discharge with a low thunder body, five takes.
- Chain collision: brief broken crackle and resonant electric snap, five takes. Only shooter/charged-marble collisions trigger it; unrelated contacts retain normal clinks.
- Charged ring exit: thunder crack with a rolling low-frequency tail, five takes.

The generated variations use −0.30, −0.15, 0, +0.15 and +0.30 semitones plus independently seeded crackle. Playback avoids the immediately previous take and preserves playback rate 1. Short baked reflections add space without continuous buzzing. Mono 44.1 kHz/16-bit WAV, faded endpoints, peak headroom (0.32 aim, 0.72 launch/arc, 0.58 thunder).

Loading and playback require both ownership and equipment of `shooter_steel_ball` in Jolens Blast. Unequipping silences active voices. Existing master/effects volume, mute, pause, tab hiding and reset apply. Collision sounds share a 55 ms throttle; ring-exit thunder shares a 90 ms throttle. The mixer caps simultaneous voices at 12 and uses its existing compressor.

Launch and chain hits include a sharp broadband crack and fixed 92/184/370/740 Hz impact resonances, with gentle saturation to retain body behind the transient. Launch gain scales from 0.6 to 1; chain hits from 0.45 to 1, keeping softer contacts audible while preserving impact strength. Normal skin sounds are unchanged.
