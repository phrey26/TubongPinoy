Jolens Blast

Win marbles by knocking them out. End your session to score 50 points for each marble you own.

Welcome to Jolens Blast!

Jolens are glass marbles. Bring a little Filipino playground rivalry to the circle—aim carefully and grow your collection!

  1. Choose your stake. Start with 10 marbles. After pressing Play, use the arrows to stake 1–5 marbles. Keep one as your shooter; the circle receives twice your stake.
  2. Aim and release. Press and hold the shooter below the circle, pull back, then release. A longer pull makes a stronger shot. You can use a mouse or touch.
  3. Knock them clear. A target must leave the circle completely. Wait for everything to stop: keep your shooter outside to collect your knockouts. If it stays inside, you lose one marble and your knockouts return.
  4. Choose when to finish. Clear the circle to place another stake, or end your session between shots. Each marble you own at session end is worth 50 points.

Each confirmed round costs 20 Juice. Shots within that round are free, even at zero Juice. Opening Play or adjusting your stake costs nothing.
