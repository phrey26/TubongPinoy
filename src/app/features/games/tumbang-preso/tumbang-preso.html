Welcome to Tumbang Preso!

A slipper, a tin can, and a little diskarte—clever thinking. Bring the Filipino street-game spirit to this aiming challenge!

  1. Aim your slipper. Click and hold the pink slipper at the bottom of the board.
  2. Pull back, then release. Pull downward to shoot upward. Pull sideways to change your angle; a longer pull gives more power. Follow the dotted aiming guide.
  3. Find your way to the can. Rocks and sidewalks bounce your slipper. Use bank shots to reach the can; leaving the top or bottom, or stopping short, counts as a miss.
  4. Keep the game going. Each hit earns 100 points and unlocks the next level. A retry costs 20 Juice. Your last 20 buys a full attempt—even when the meter reaches zero.

Starting this game is free. Juice is only spent when you choose Try Again after a miss.
