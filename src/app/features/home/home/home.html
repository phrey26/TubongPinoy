About Tubong Pinoy

Tubong Pinoy is a web-based casual gaming platform designed to bring traditional Filipino-inspired games into the modern digital space. Built using Angular and MongoDB, the platform allows users to instantly play games directly from their browser.

The system focuses on accessibility, simplicity, and engagement—making it suitable for both children and adults. Players can create accounts, track their progress, and enjoy interactive drag-and-shoot gameplay mechanics.

By blending cultural inspiration with modern web technologies, Tubong Pinoy preserves classic gameplay experiences while adapting them for today’s digital generation.
