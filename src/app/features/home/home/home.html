About Tubong Pinoy

Before a screen became a playground, an afternoon could begin with a pair of slippers, a handful of marbles, and someone calling you outside. There were dusty feet, cheers over a lucky shot, and pleas for one more round before it was time to go home. Tubong Pinoy is a little love letter to those afternoons.

For adults, we hope each shot brings back a familiar feeling—and perhaps the memory of an old playmate. For a new generation, this is a small window into the games their parents and grandparents grew up with: a chance to discover the fun, ask about the stories, and maybe try a round together outside.

New to Filipino street games? You’re welcome here. Tumbang Preso centers on knocking down a tin can with a thrown slipper. Our version turns that challenge into an aiming puzzle: pull back, release, and find a path past the rocks to hit the can. Jolens means glass marbles. In Jolens Blast, aim your shooter marble to knock the others out of the circle. Keep your shooter outside the ring to collect your knockouts; if it stays inside, you lose a marble and the knocked-out targets return.

Choose a game and play right in your browser—no download needed. Drag farther for a stronger shot, adjust your angle, and release when you’re ready. Keep an eye on your “Juice,” the energy used for Tumbang Preso retries and new Jolens Blast rounds. These are playful adaptations of outdoor games, with a little of that old neighborhood spirit waiting in every round. Whether you remember the street or are discovering it for the first time, tara, laro tayo—come on, let’s play!
